Our services

Everything electrical, under one accountable team.

Design and consultation, supply, installation, approvals, testing, commissioning and maintenance — for domestic, commercial, industrial and agricultural clients.

01 — HIGH TENSION

HT Installations & Substations

Complete turnkey HT services for buildings and plants that need a dedicated transformer. We have executed HT installations from 300 kVA showrooms to a 3000 kVA metals plant.

  • Load assessment and transformer sizing
  • Indoor & outdoor substations, transformer yards and plinths
  • HT VCB panels, RMUs, HT metering cubicles
  • HT cable laying, terminations and jointing
  • Earthing stations and lightning protection
  • DG set synchronisation and changeover systems
  • Drawing approval from CEIG, Chennai and coordination with TANGEDCO for energisation

Selected HT projects: OPG Metals (3000 kVA), Prist Medical College (2000 kVA), Aravind Eye Hospital (1250 kVA), Seematti Silks Thiruvarur (1000 kVA), Anantham Silks Rajapalayam & Kumbakonam (1000 kVA), Prist University (1000 kVA).

02 — LOW TENSION

LT Electrical Works

From a single bungalow to a multi-floor hospital, we deliver neat, safe and well-documented LT installations that are easy to maintain for decades.

  • Internal & external wiring, conduiting and cable trays
  • Main and sub-distribution, rising mains and bus ducts
  • Architectural, showroom and façade lighting
  • LT-CT metered services up to the LT supply limit
  • Earthing, surge protection and lightning arrestors
  • UPS, inverter and DG backup integration
  • Home & building automation
03 — PANELS

Panel Boards & Power Factor Correction

We supply and erect all types of panel boards — LT, HT and Automatic Power Factor Control (APFC) panels — built with switchgear from our quality partners.

  • Main LT panels, PCC and MCC panels
  • Feeder pillars, sub-distribution boards
  • APFC panels to hold power factor and avoid penalties
  • AMF / changeover panels for DG sets
  • Metering & energy monitoring panels
  • Retrofit and upgrade of existing panels

04 — COMPLIANCE

CEIG Approvals & Liaisoning

Statutory approvals are where many projects lose weeks. Our dedicated liaisoning team handles the paperwork so your building can be energised on time.

  • Electrical drawings prepared to statutory requirements
  • Drawing approval from the CEIG, Chennai for HT jobs
  • Inspection coordination and safety certificates
  • New service connections and load enhancement with TANGEDCO
  • Documentation handover for your records

Read more: CEIG drawing approval — what building owners should know →

05 — LIFTS

Lift & Escalator Licensing

Lifts and escalators must be licensed before use and kept licensed thereafter. We obtain licences for new lifts and escalators and handle licence renewals for existing installations.

  • Licence applications for new lifts and escalators
  • Licence renewal for existing installations
  • Coordination of inspections with the Electrical Inspectorate
  • Power supply, earthing and lighting for lift wells and machine rooms
06 — LOW VOLTAGE

CCTV, Security, Intercom & Networking

Planned alongside your electrical layout, so cameras, intercoms and network points are powered, protected and neatly concealed from day one.

  • CCTV surveillance & security systems
  • Intercom facilities for homes, hotels and offices
  • Structured cabling & data networking
  • Integration with backup power
07 — SUPPORT

AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract)

Our Annual Maintenance Contracts keep showrooms trading, hospitals running and campuses powered. Preventive maintenance stops small faults becoming expensive shutdowns.

  • Breakdown attendance round the clock
  • Annual maintenance contracts for HT & LT installations
  • Transformer & panel preventive maintenance
  • Thermal checks, earth resistance testing and load balancing
08 — RENEWABLES & SUPPLY

Solar Works & Electrical Supply

We have executed electrical works for a 5.5 MW solar power plant at Palladam, and supply quality electrical goods at the lowest viable cost for projects of every size.

  • Solar plant electrical erection (AC side, cabling, earthing)
  • Rooftop solar integration with existing LT systems
  • Supply of branded cables, switchgear, fittings and panels
