About the company

What does an 'EA' Grade electrical contractor licence mean?

'EA' is the highest grade of electrical contractor licence issued by the Electrical Licensing Board, Tamil Nadu. It authorises the holder to execute electrical installations at all voltage levels — including high tension (HT) services, substations and transformer installations — in addition to all low tension (LT) work.

Services & scope

Do you handle both HT and LT installations?

Yes. We execute HT installations — transformers, HT panels, RMUs, cabling and substations (we have built up to 3000 kVA) — as well as complete LT works including wiring, distribution, lighting, earthing and LT-CT metering.

Do you supply panel boards?

Yes — we supply and erect all types of panel boards: LT panels, HT panels and Automatic Power Factor Control (APFC) panels, built with switchgear from brands such as Schneider, Siemens, L&T, Legrand, Havells and Mitsubishi.

Can you work from our architect's or consultant's drawings?

Absolutely. Most of our commercial projects are executed to drawings from the project's architect and electrical consultant. We can also prepare the electrical design ourselves for smaller projects.

Do you also do CCTV, intercom and networking?

Yes. We provide CCTV and security systems, intercom facilities and data networking — planned together with your electrical layout.

Do you take up residential projects?

Yes — from individual bungalows (including fully automated homes) to multi-storey apartment complexes.

Do you work on solar projects?

Yes. We have executed electrical works for a 5.5 MW solar power plant at Palladam, and can integrate rooftop solar with your existing LT installation.

Approvals & compliance

Can you obtain CEIG drawing approval for our HT service?

Yes. We prepare the drawings and documents, obtain drawing approval from the CEIG (Chief Electrical Inspector to Government), Chennai for HT jobs, and coordinate the inspection and energisation. Read our guide.

Do I need an HT connection or will LT be enough?

It depends on your connected load and the supply limits set by the regulator and TANGEDCO. Smaller buildings are usually served at LT (or LT-CT); larger showrooms, hospitals and campuses typically need an HT service with their own transformer. Our article explains the trade-offs, and we are happy to assess your load.

Do you handle lift and escalator licences?

Yes — we obtain licences for new lifts and escalators and handle licence renewals for existing installations.

Will you liaise with TANGEDCO for a new service connection?

Yes. Our liaisoning team handles new service applications, load enhancement and related coordination with TANGEDCO.

Quotes & process

How do I get a quotation?

Call us on +91 996 505 6195, email bhabuelectricals@gmail.com or use the enquiry form. Share drawings if you have them; otherwise we will arrange a site visit to understand your requirement before quoting.

What information should I share for an accurate estimate?

Architectural and electrical drawings (if available), the building's built-up area and use, the expected connected load, preferred brands, backup requirements (DG/UPS/solar) and your target completion date.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary with scope. A showroom or hotel fit-out is often completed in a few weeks to a few months; HT substations and large campuses take longer and depend partly on statutory approvals. We share a schedule with milestones at the start of every project.

Which brands do you use?

We use only proven, branded materials from our quality partners — including Schneider Electric, Siemens, L&T / Lauritz Knudsen, Legrand, Havells, Anchor by Panasonic, Mitsubishi Electric, KEI, RR Kabel, OBO Bettermann and Kirloskar — or the makes specified by your consultant.

Maintenance & support

Do you offer maintenance after the project is handed over?

Yes. We provide AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts), covering preventive maintenance and annual maintenance contracts for HT and LT installations.

Can you take over maintenance of an installation done by someone else?

Yes. We will first inspect and test the installation, share a report of any safety or compliance gaps, and then take it under maintenance.

What should I do in an electrical emergency?

If it is safe to do so, switch off the main supply, keep people away from the affected area and call us on +91 996 505 6195 or +91 944 310 6343. For fire or electric shock, call emergency services (112) first.
