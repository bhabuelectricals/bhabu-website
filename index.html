What does an 'EA' Grade electrical contractor licence mean?

'EA' is the highest grade of electrical contractor licence issued by the Electrical Licensing Board, Tamil Nadu. It authorises us to carry out electrical installation work at all voltage levels — including HT installations, substations and transformers.

Which areas do you work in?

We are headquartered in Kumbakonam and regularly execute projects across Tamil Nadu — Thanjavur, Trichy, Mayiladuthurai, Karaikudi, Ramanathapuram, Cuddalore, Coimbatore, Salem, Chennai and more.

Can you handle the CEIG approval for our HT service?

Yes. We prepare the drawings and documentation, obtain drawing approval from the CEIG, Chennai, and coordinate inspection and energisation with TANGEDCO.

Do you provide maintenance after handover?

Yes — we offer AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts) with periodic preventive maintenance and annual maintenance contracts for HT and LT installations.
