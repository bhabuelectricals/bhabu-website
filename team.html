Managing Director · B.Tech., MBA

An engineer with more than three decades of hands-on project experience, Er. S. Kannan leads Bhabu Electricals' engineering, client relationships and quality culture.

Bhabu Electricals is a Proud Member of the TamilNadu Electrical Installation Engineers Association 'A' Grade and of BMO, and holds the post of Secretary of the Tamilnadu Electrical Consultants and Contractors Association.
