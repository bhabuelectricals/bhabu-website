About us

36 years of building Tamil Nadu's electrical backbone.

Domestic, commercial, industrial or institutional — Bhabu Electricals is fully committed to professional, safe and affordable electrical services, from design and consultation through to commissioning.

Who we are

Experienced professionals who get the job done right.

Electrical work can be dangerous and should only be carried out by registered electricians with the right skills and tools. Our electricians are highly experienced, fully qualified and fully insured — so you can rely on us to do the work safely, professionally and with care.

In operation since 1990, we are one of the most experienced electrical contractors in the region. We have successfully completed electrical installation and testing for domestic, industrial and commercial projects of every size — from temple lighting and private bungalows to 2000 kVA medical college campuses.

It is our continued investment in training our technicians that allows us to ensure every job is carried out to the highest standard. Our aim for quality and our dedication to top-notch customer service are what make us the electrical company of choice for architects, consultants and owners across the state.
